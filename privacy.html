Privacy & cookies

Short version: I collect as little as possible, I do not sell anything to anyone, and you can ask me to delete your information at any time.

Who is responsible

Shiny Minds Coaching is responsible for the personal information described here. You can reach us through the booking link on this site or by message on Instagram @shinymindscoaching.

What is collected

  • Booking information. When you book a free chat, Calendly collects your name, email address and anything you choose to write. That is used to arrange and hold the conversation.
  • Coaching notes. If we work together, brief notes are kept so sessions build on each other. They are stored securely, kept to what is useful, and not shared.
  • Website analytics. If you accept cookies, Google Analytics records anonymised information about pages visited, approximate location and device type. It is used only to understand which advice helps parents most.

Children’s information

Parents and carers make all bookings and decisions. Information about a child is only recorded with a parent’s knowledge, and children’s stories or images are only shared publicly with written consent. Nothing about a child is ever used for marketing.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies unless you press “Accept” on the cookie notice. If you decline, analytics cookies are not stored. Your choice is saved in your browser so you are not asked again, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Who else is involved

A few trusted services process data on our behalf: Google Analytics (website statistics), Calendly (booking), Instagram and GitHub Pages (hosting). Each has its own privacy policy and its own safeguards. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long it is kept

Booking messages are kept only as long as needed to arrange the conversation. Coaching notes are kept while we work together and for a short period afterwards, then deleted. Analytics data is retained for the period set in Google Analytics.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information held about you or your child, and you can withdraw consent for analytics at any time. Ask us and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how a request is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
